# Security policy

Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub: **Security → Report a vulnerability** on
this repository. Please do not open a public issue.

We acknowledge reports within 7 days during the beta and aim to fix critical issues within
14 days. We credit reporters in the advisory unless they prefer not to be named.

In scope: this repository's code (the engine, the parsers, the player and the CLI), the hosted service
at https://gource.online (its clone service, edge and storage) and the player at
https://viewer.gource.online. Out of scope: denial of service through volume, and social engineering.
